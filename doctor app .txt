# 1. DOCUMENT PURPOSE

This document defines the proposed functional, technical and operational scope of the **CareID Doctor Mobile Application**.

The Doctor App is an **Expanded Clinical Companion** that enables authorized doctors to perform daily clinical activities, review consultations, review treatment offers, participate in clinical clearance workflows, book appointments and procedural slots, and communicate with patients or office teams through supported workflows.

The Doctor App is **not intended to duplicate every feature of Doctor Web**.

The application must operate using the existing CareID Backend, shared data and Admin-managed configuration.

## 1.1 Document Authority

The governing requirement sources are:

1. Original HEALTH VAULT 360 PRD/FSD.
2. Approved CareID change requests, CR-02 through CR-08.
3. Approved CareID architectural and security decisions.
4. This Doctor App specification, after stakeholder approval.

**Conflict rule:** If this document conflicts with an explicit requirement in the original PRD/FSD or approved change requests, the original approved requirement takes precedence until a formal change is approved.

**Verification limitation:** The original PRD/FSD and change-request workbook were not available for direct inspection while preparing this version. Therefore, this document distinguishes established project decisions from proposed mobile design and items requiring source verification.

No unverified proposal should be treated as an already-approved requirement.

# 2. PRODUCT VISION

## 2.1 Product Definition

CareID Doctor App is a secure mobile clinical workspace for authorized human healthcare doctors and veterinary practitioners.

It enables doctors to access relevant clinical information and perform supported actions without requiring continuous access to a desktop workstation.

## 2.2 Product Principles

1. **Mobile-first usability:** Prioritize frequent, time-sensitive actions.
2. **Shared Backend:** Doctor App and Doctor Web must use consistent records and business rules.
3. **Dynamic configuration:** Configurable operational values must be managed through authorized Admin workflows, not duplicated as hardcoded frontend or Backend lists.
4. **Clinical safety:** Preserve authorization, clinical clearance and patient identity checks.
5. **Human/veterinary isolation:** Maintain strict separation between human and veterinary clinical workspaces.
6. **Scope control:** Implement only documented or explicitly approved features.
7. **Reliable persistence:** Changes must survive refresh, logout, login and application restart.
8. **Transparent status:** Never display an action as completed until the Backend confirms it.

# 4. USER ROLES AND ACCESS

## 4.1 Supported Clinical Personas

### Human Healthcare Doctor

Access to authorized human patient workflows, appointments, consultations, prescriptions, referrals, clinical records and treatment processes.

### Veterinary Practitioner

Access to authorized animal patient workflows, pet records, guardians, vaccinations, appointments and veterinary clinical actions.

## 4.2 Clinical Practice Classification

Clinical practice type must be obtained from authoritative Backend data.

The application must not infer clinical scope solely from a specialty name or a frontend keyword list.

A valid single-scope clinician must be routed to the appropriate workspace.

Missing, contradictory or unauthorized clinical scope must trigger a safe access-blocking state.

## 4.3 Authorization

Every sensitive Backend operation must enforce:

- Authenticated identity.
- Appropriate role and permission.
- Patient or animal record access.
- Facility affiliation where required.
- Clinical practice scope.
- Workflow-specific authorization.

Hiding a button in Flutter is not a substitute for Backend authorization.

## DA-07 — TREATMENT OFFER REVIEW

### Purpose

Allow doctors to review and track treatment offers created through the approved CareID consultation workflow.

### Proposed Mobile Capabilities

- View treatment offer list.
- Open offer details.
- View patient and procedure.
- View facility and baseline price.
- View discounts and financial summary.
- View selected financing option.
- View offer expiration.
- View clinical review status.
- View patient acceptance, rejection or pending response.
- View offer history.
- Perform explicitly authorized offer actions.

### Web-First Boundary

Complex treatment-offer creation, advanced pricing calculations and extensive financial editing should remain in Doctor Web unless the approved source documents require them in the Doctor App.

### Dynamic Data Requirements

Offer information must be derived from:

- Procedure catalogue.
- Facility-specific chargemaster.
- Facility currency.
- Approved financing packages.
- Offer validity policies.
- Clinical remarks and configured requirements.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Mobile financial totals match authoritative Backend calculations.
- Missing required facility prices are not silently replaced by unrelated default prices.
- An expired offer is not displayed as currently valid.
- The app distinguishes draft, dispatched, accepted, declined and other supported lifecycle states.
- Patient acceptance must be a real Backend-confirmed event, not a simulated UI action.

## DA-08 — CLINICAL CLEARANCE

### Purpose

Allow authorized clinicians to review clinical readiness and perform permitted clearance actions.

### Proposed Capabilities

- View pre-treatment requirements.
- View clinical review status.
- View required laboratory and diagnostic evidence.
- View anesthesia or specialist clearance status.
- View unresolved clinical holds.
- Open linked supporting records.
- Perform permitted clearance decisions.
- View reviewer identity, time and decision history.

### Critical Safety Requirements

A clearance must not be marked complete merely because a user taps a button.

Where the approved workflow requires evidence or authorization, the Backend must validate those conditions.

The app must not:

- Auto-approve missing clinical evidence.
- Bypass required reviews.
- Allow unauthorized users to clear holds.
- Convert a UI toast into a completed clearance state without persistence.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Clearance state persists after app restart.
- Backend and Doctor Web reflect the same state.
- An unresolved mandatory hold blocks dependent actions.
- Approval history is auditable.
- Failed updates show a meaningful error without falsely displaying success.

# 7. QR-BASED PATIENT INTERACTION

## 7.1 Requirement Status

**Mandatory documentation reconciliation item.**

The user has confirmed that Patient App QR scanning is required by the original documentation.

The exact QR workflow must be verified against the original PRD/FSD before its interaction contract is finalized.

## 7.2 Integration Requirements

The Doctor App must support its documented role in the QR-based patient interaction workflow.

The implementation must identify, from the original source:

- Which application generates or displays the QR code.
- Which application scans the QR code.
- Whether scanning occurs during registration, identification, consultation, appointment check-in or another documented stage.
- Which Backend operation is triggered.
- Which patient or appointment context is established.
- What permissions or consent are required.
- What happens after successful scanning.
- How expired, invalid, reused or unauthorized QR codes are handled.

## 7.3 Proposed Security Baseline

Subject to alignment with the documented workflow:

- QR content must not be treated as proof of authorization by itself.
- The Backend must validate any patient or appointment reference.
- QR data must not grant access to unrelated patient records.
- Sensitive patient information should not be unnecessarily exposed in QR payloads.
- Invalid or expired QR interactions must fail safely.
- Successful scans should be auditable where appropriate.

## 7.4 Acceptance Gate

**QR-GATE-01:** No final QR scanning flow may be declared complete until the original documented sequence has been mapped to Patient App, Doctor App and Backend responsibilities and verified end to end.

# 13. CONSULTATION-TO-BOOKING LIFECYCLE

The proposed Doctor App must reflect the same authoritative lifecycle as Doctor Web.

### Stage 1 — Patient Context

Identify the authorized patient and relevant consultation.

Use the documented QR workflow at the applicable stage once verified.

### Stage 2 — Clinical Review

Review relevant records, warnings, consultation notes and clinical requirements.

### Stage 3 — Treatment Offer

Review procedure, facility price, financing details, offer validity and current status.

### Stage 4 — Patient Response

Track the patient's actual offer response through the Backend.

Do not simulate acceptance within Doctor App.

### Stage 5 — Clinical Clearance

Review required evidence, outstanding holds and authorized clinical decisions.

### Stage 6 — Booking Eligibility

The Backend evaluates applicable requirements and confirms whether booking is permitted.

### Stage 7 — Slot Selection

Retrieve real available appointment or procedural slots.

### Stage 8 — Booking Confirmation

Confirm the selected slot through the Backend and persist the booking.

### Stage 9 — Follow-Up

Show appointment status, pending tasks and relevant notifications.

**Scope rule:** The exact required sequence, gates and actions must be reconciled with the original PRD/FSD. The Doctor App must not introduce new mandatory clinical gates or financing obligations without approval.

# 18. DEFINITION OF DONE

A Doctor App feature is complete only when:

- Its requirement is approved and traceable.
- The Flutter screen is implemented.
- The relevant Backend contract is integrated.
- Authentication and authorization are enforced.
- Required data is dynamically loaded.
- User actions persist correctly.
- Loading, error and empty states are handled.
- Cross-platform consistency is verified where relevant.
- Relevant negative and security tests pass.
- No hardcoded operational configuration is introduced.
- Evidence is recorded.
- No unrelated application or workspace is modified.

A compiled application is not automatically a functionally complete application.

# 22. FINAL PRODUCT BOUNDARY

**CareID Doctor App is an Expanded Clinical Companion, not a complete replacement for Doctor Web.**

It must enable approved mobile clinical activities, including:

- Patient and appointment access.
- Clinical record review.
- Supported consultation actions.
- Treatment-offer review and tracking.
- Authorized clinical clearance actions.
- Eligible appointment and procedural slot booking.
- Prescriptions and referrals.
- Office Chat.
- Notifications.
- Documented QR-based Patient App interaction.
- Separate human and veterinary workspaces.

All operational data and business rules must remain consistent with the authoritative CareID Backend and Admin-managed configuration.

**No new clinical, financial, administrative or interoperability capability may be introduced solely on developer assumption.**
