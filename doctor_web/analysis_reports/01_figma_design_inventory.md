## 3. Structural Breakdown by Clinical Domain

### Domain 1: Authentication & Identity Security (Frames 1:2, 1:79)
- **Login (`1:2`):** Split-column 1280×873 layout. Left column (576px) contains floating rounded card (480×601) with country prefix selector, tel input, CTA button, encrypted access badge, and compliance footer. Right column (704px) displays CareID cloud brand graphic with clinical value propositions.
- **OTP Verification (`1:79`):** Split-column 1280×1024 layout. Dedicated standalone card with integrated `CareIDlogo 1` header, 6 individual digit input boxes, resend countdown timer (00:28), and full-bleed high-contrast navy security hero graphic with 3D DNA/shield artwork.

### Domain 2: Clinical Dashboard & Directory Search (Frames 1:154, 1:6657, 1:745)
- **Dashboard (`1:154`):** Doctor greeting ("Good Morning, Dr. Julian Vance"), clinical telemetry status, quick CTA buttons ("Add New Patient", "Schedule Appointment"), 4 KPI stat tiles (Today's Appointments, Pending Lab Reviews, Critical Alerts, Avg. Consult Time), 5-row interactive appointment schedule table, and real-time active telemetry ribbon.
- **Patient Directory (`1:6657`):** Multi-attribute search (Name, ID, Mobile, DOB), demographic filters (Age, Gender, Blood Group), 4-row patient registry table with Health Vault IDs, status badges (Active, Critical, Waiting, In Consultation), and action menus.
- **Refined Search Empty State (`1:745`):** Dedicated "No Results" state with query tags, search recommendations ("Try searching using Health Vault ID, Patient Name, Mobile Number, or MRN"), and secondary restoration CTA ("Clear Filters", "Back to Directory").

### Domain 3: Patient Longitudinal Record & Financing (Frames 1:473, 1:5316)
- **Patient Profile (`1:473`):** Longitudinal EHR cockpit with biometrics summary header (Blood Group, Age, Gender, Primary Physician Dr. Julian Vance), allergy tags (Penicillin), active telemetry ribbons (BP 120/80, HR 72bpm), clinical tab navigation (Overview, Medical History, Vitals, Records, Encounters), and recent clinical visits.
- **Financing Hub (`1:5316`):** Multi-tier procedural financing calculator (Tier 1 0% APR, Tier 2 Standard, Tier 3 Extended), interactive down-payment slider, monthly payment matrix, underwriting risk indicator, and credit approval ratification.

### Domain 4: Diagnostic Records & Specialized Viewers (Frames 1:12893, 1:13265, 1:6023, 1:5719, 1:6396, 1:882, 1:2908, 1:3203)
- **Medical Records Registry (`1:12893`):** 1576px wide master records index with specialty tabs (Medical, Dental, Vision), date range picker, clinic facility filter, and record timeline.
- **Prescription Builder / SOAP Form (`1:13265`):** 1576px clinical documentation form with Chief Complaint ("Hypertension follow-up"), Diagnosis, Current Medications, and multi-field Prescription Builder (Medication Name, Strength, Route, Dosage, Frequency, Duration, Quantity).
- **Refined Document Viewer (`1:5719`):** High-resolution document viewer modal with toolbar (Zoom, Pan, Rotate, Invert, Download, Print, Close).
- **Laboratory Report Viewer (`1:2908`):** Complete CMP / Hematology panel viewer with collected date, result date, reference ranges, and critical out-of-range indicators.
- **Radiology Viewer & Imaging (`1:882`, `1:3203`):** Multi-planar DICOM imaging viewer with window/level sliders, crosshair annotations, patient clinical indication summary, and radiologist sign-off attestation.
- **Prescription & Clinical History (`1:6396`):** Active electronic prescriptions list, dosage instructions, refills remaining, prescriber attribution, and dispensing status.

### Domain 5: Neural OCR Ingestion Pipeline (Frames 1:1167, 1:1088, 1:1325, 1:1513)
- **Initial Upload (`1:1167`):** Drag-and-drop zone with camera capture trigger, file format selectors (PDF, PNG, JPG, DICOM), and recent upload history.
- **Processing State (`1:1088`):** Multi-stage progress indicator (Ingesting Document -> Neural Character Recognition -> Clinical Entity Extraction -> EHR Cross-Verification).
- **OCR Field Verification (`1:1325`):** Side-by-side verification interface: left side displays scanned document with interactive SVG bounding boxes; right side displays editable extracted fields with confidence scores.
- **Upload Success (`1:1513`):** Confirmation screen with linked EHR patient record, audit hash, and action CTAs ("View in Medical Records", "Upload Another").

### Domain 6: Specialist Referrals Network (Frames 1:1535, 1:1829, 1:2072, 1:2343, 1:2559)
- **Referral Dashboard (`1:1535`):** Metric tiles (Active Referrals, Outgoing, Incoming, Urgency Rating), searchable referral table, and referral status chips.
- **Create Referral (`1:1829`):** Specialist selector, urgency scoring (Routine, Urgent, Emergency), clinical summary builder, and document attachment drawer.
- **Referral Details (`1:2072`):** Longitudinal dossier with full clinical indication, referring doctor credentials, attached imaging links, and consultation decision actions.
- **Incoming Referral Review (`1:2343`):** Triage workflow for reviewing incoming clinical packages, accepting or redirecting referrals, and staging consultation slots.
- **Referral Success (`1:2559`):** Success confirmation with referral tracking token and notification dispatch confirmation.

### Domain 7: Clinical Reports & Analytics (Frames 1:2590, 1:3732)
- **Reports Dashboard (`1:2590`):** Daily diagnostic metrics, pending review queues, and category breakdown.
- **Critical Report Review (`1:3732`):** Emergency diagnostic alert viewer for out-of-bounds lab/imaging results with immediate clinical triage actions (Stage Emergency Consultation, Contact Referring Physician, Acknowledge Alert).

### Domain 8: Notification Center & Clinician Settings (Frames 1:3950, 1:4281, 1:4531, 1:4834, 1:5113)
- **Notification Center (`1:3950`):** Categorized notification feed (All, Critical Alerts, Referrals, OCR Ingestion, System Updates) with mark-as-read and filter toggles.
- **Professional Profile (`1:4281`):** Medical license information, primary affiliations, consultation types, biography, and credentials.
- **Account & Security Settings (`1:4531`):** Two-factor authentication (TOTP/SMS), active clinical workstation sessions, password change, and audit log.
- **Notification Preferences (`1:4834`):** Granular channel toggles (SMS, Push, Email) for critical vs non-critical alerts with digest frequency.
- **Support & Help Center (`1:5113`):** Clinical workflow documentation, HIPAA compliance guidelines, and IT helpdesk ticketing interface.

  6. `1:9573` Treatment Cleared to Proceed (Clinical handoff & anesthesia clearance)
  7. `1:10206` Schedule Clinical Slot (Cath Lab suite selection, date/time matrix)
  8. `1:10786` Slot Confirmation & Final Review (Locking clinical team & equipment)
  9. `1:11274` Procedure Scheduled Successfully (Dispatched prep & fasting instructions)
  10. `1:11774` Scheduled Procedure & Appointment Details (Cath Lab Suite 3 booking details)
  11. `1:12355` Pre-Procedure Readiness Checklist (Coagulation labs, NPO status, consent)
  12. `1:13596` Day-of-Procedure Check-In (Patient arrival, vitals re-check, gowning)
  13. `1:14096` Patient & Clinical Safety Verification (WHO Surgical Time-Out, allergy check)
  14. `1:14597` Start Clinical Encounter (Fluoroscopy clock initiation, induction)
